# UX Principles (MVP)

## Product Feel

- Focus is a fragile commitment.
- Break is a safe release.
- Return is intentional re-entry.

## Behavioral Design Rules

- During focus: friction against accidental app exits.
- During break: permit disengagement without guilt.
- After break: invite, never force, re-entry.

## Notification Tone

- Destabilization notification: concise and urgent.
- Break complete notification: calm invitation to return.
- Avoid spammy repetition or punitive messaging.

## Guardrails

- Never auto-start a new focus session.
- Do not use manipulative streak pressure in MVP.
- Keep emotional tone supportive and respectful.
